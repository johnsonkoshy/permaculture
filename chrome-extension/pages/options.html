Open the group's new post or event form, then click Fill again on the helper's panel if it didn't fill by itself.

How it works

  1. In the Event Hub, choose Open & fill next to a site.
  2. The helper opens that site in a new tab and fills in the title, date, time, place, description and image wherever it can find the boxes.
  3. A small panel on the page lists every field with a Copy button, for anything it couldn't fill.
  4. You check everything and click Publish yourself. The helper never publishes.

Privacy

The event you send stays in this browser. The helper has no server, sends nothing anywhere, and only runs on the Event Hub, Partiful, Luma, Meetup, Facebook and Hylo. Your group links are saved in your Chrome profile.
