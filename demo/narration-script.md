## Recording tips

- Play the no-voice video while you record, so your timing follows the screen.
- Save the recording as one file (.m4a, .wav or .mp3) starting at 0:00. I can mix it in and check the sync.
- Some windows are longer than their lines: 0:45–1:03, 1:53–2:03 and 2:08–2:17. You can add a sentence there.
